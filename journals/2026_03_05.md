- In life, we often experience profound events that shape us as beings, and as a result we feel the need to hold onto some form of physical reminder of that event. Often, these are photographs, souvenirs, nick-knacks, and more. Such objects often evoke a strong emotional response, harkening back to the associated moments. For many, parting with such objects are devastating. For some, grief or distress rises when these items are lost, broken, or damaged. Sometimes, items that have no emotional ties provoke these feelings too -- such as the far to common experience of 'losing ones keys'. Through the teachings of the Dharma, it is possible to learn to overcome such suffering, and uproot the origin of it entirely. To understand how, one must fist understand a central truth of the Dharma.
